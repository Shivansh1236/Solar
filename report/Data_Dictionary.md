# Solar Power Generation Data Dictionary

## Dataset Overview

This project uses operational and weather data collected from two solar power plants in India.

### Files

- Plant_1_Generation_Data.csv
- Plant_1_Weather_Sensor_Data.csv
- Plant_2_Generation_Data.csv
- Plant_2_Weather_Sensor_Data.csv

The dataset contains:
- Power generation measurements
- Weather sensor readings
- Inverter-level production
- Time-series observations recorded at regular intervals


#### 1. Generation Data

**File Name:** `Plant_1_Generation_Data.csv`

| Column Name | Data Type | Description | Example |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| DATE_TIME | DateTime | Timestamp when the data was recorded | 15-05-2020 00:00 |
| PLANT_ID | Integer | Unique identifier for the solar power plant | 4135001 |
| SOURCE_KEY | String | Unique identifier for the inverter | 1BY6WEcLGh8j5v7 |
| DC_POWER | Float | Direct Current (DC) power generated by the inverter (kW) | 0.00 |
| AC_POWER | Float | Alternating Current (AC) power output after conversion (kW) | 0.00 |
| DAILY_YIELD | Float | Energy generated by the inverter during the current day (kWh) | 0.00 |
| TOTAL_YIELD | Float | Total cumulative energy generated since installation (kWh) | 6259559 |

---

##### Column Descriptions

###### DATE_TIME
- Stores the exact date and time of the observation.
- Used for time-series analysis and trend visualization.

###### PLANT_ID
- Unique identification number for the solar power plant.
- Useful when working with multiple plants.

###### SOURCE_KEY
- Unique identifier of each inverter.
- Every inverter has its own generation data.

###### DC_POWER
- Power generated by the solar panels before conversion.
- Measured in kilowatts (kW).

###### AC_POWER
- Output power after the inverter converts DC to AC.
- Usually slightly lower than DC power due to conversion losses.

###### DAILY_YIELD
- Total energy generated by the inverter during the current day.
- Resets every day.

###### TOTAL_YIELD
- Total cumulative energy generated by the inverter.
- Never resets and continually increases.

#### 2. Weather Sensor Data

**File Name:** `Plant_1_Weather_Sensor_Data.csv`

| Column Name | Data Type | Description | Example |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| DATE_TIME | DateTime | Timestamp of weather observation | 15-05-2020 00:00 |
| PLANT_ID | Integer | Solar plant identifier | 4135001 |
| SOURCE_KEY | String | Weather sensor identifier | HmiyD2TTLFNqkNe |
| AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE | Float | Air temperature around the solar panels (°C) | 25.18 |
| MODULE_TEMPERATURE | Float | Surface temperature of the solar panel (°C) | 22.86 |
| IRRADIATION | Float | Solar radiation received by the panels (kW/m²) | 0.0 |

---

##### Column Descriptions

###### AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE
- Temperature of the surrounding environment.
- Higher temperatures can affect panel efficiency.

###### MODULE_TEMPERATURE
- Temperature of the solar panel surface.
- Usually higher than ambient temperature during sunny conditions.

###### IRRADIATION
- Amount of sunlight falling on the solar panels.
- One of the strongest predictors of solar power generation.

---

## Key Variables for Analysis

The following variables are most important for exploratory data analysis and predictive modeling:

- DC_POWER (Target/Output)
- AC_POWER
- IRRADIATION
- MODULE_TEMPERATURE
- AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE
- DATE_TIME

These variables help analyze power generation patterns and build forecasting models.
